Storm response expands across the coast

July 26, 2026

Emergency crews expanded their response after overnight storms damaged roads and power lines across several coastal communities.

Officials asked residents to avoid flooded intersections while repair teams restored service to hospitals and shelters.

What officials said

The regional emergency office said the most serious flooding had passed, but warned that rivers could rise again after afternoon rain.

Residents should use official alerts and avoid unnecessary travel.
