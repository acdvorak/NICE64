Nintendo 64

The Nintendo 64 is a home video game console developed and marketed by Nintendo. It was released in 1996 and introduced a new generation of three-dimensional games.

The console used cartridges and supported four controller ports without an external adapter.

History

Development focused on real-time graphics, controller design, and games that demonstrated new kinds of play.

  • Released in Japan in 1996
  • Released in North America in 1996
